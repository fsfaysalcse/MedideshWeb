Introducing Smart Alerts: Never Run Out of Stock Again

Faysal Hossain
CEO & Founder
October 5, 2025 8 min read

Running out of essential medicines can be devastating for your pharmacy business. Lost sales, disappointed customers, and damaged reputation are just some of the consequences. Today, we're excited to introduce Smart Alerts, a revolutionary feature that uses machine learning to predict stock levels and notify you before items run out.

The Problem with Traditional Inventory Management

Most pharmacies rely on manual stock checks or basic reorder point systems. While these methods work to some extent, they fail to account for seasonal variations, sudden demand spikes, or changing consumption patterns. This often leads to either overstocking (tying up capital) or understocking (losing sales).

Our research shows that the average pharmacy loses approximately 15-20% of potential revenue due to stockouts of fast-moving items. Additionally, expired medicine waste costs pharmacies an estimated 5-8% of their inventory value annually.

Key Insight

Pharmacies using predictive inventory management see a 30% reduction in stockouts and a 25% decrease in expired medicine waste within the first six months.

How Smart Alerts Work

Smart Alerts leverages advanced machine learning algorithms to analyze your historical sales data, seasonal trends, and consumption patterns. The system continuously learns from your pharmacy's unique behavior and adapts its predictions accordingly.

Intelligent Prediction Engine

Our prediction engine considers multiple factors when forecasting your inventory needs:

  • Historical sales data and trends over the past 12 months
  • Seasonal variations and demand patterns
  • Day-of-week and time-of-month patterns
  • Supplier lead times and delivery schedules
  • Product shelf life and expiration dates

Multi-Channel Notifications

When the system detects that an item will likely run out before the next scheduled reorder, it sends you alerts through multiple channels:

In-App Alerts

Dashboard notifications with recommended order quantities

Email Reports

Daily or weekly summaries of items requiring attention

SMS Alerts

Critical notifications for high-priority medicines
