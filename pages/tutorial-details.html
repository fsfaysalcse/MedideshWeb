Complete Setup Guide: Getting Started with MediDesh

Beginner
12:45 minutes
8,234 views
Last updated: Oct 2025

Learn how to set up your pharmacy from scratch, configure essential settings, and process your first sale. This comprehensive guide will walk you through every step needed to get MediDesh running smoothly in your pharmacy.

Tutorial Steps

1

Download and Install MediDesh

Get started by downloading MediDesh for your preferred platform. We support Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, and iOS devices. Choose the version that matches your operating system and hardware requirements.

System Requirements
Minimum 4GB RAM, 2GB free disk space, and a stable internet connection. For optimal performance, we recommend 8GB RAM and an SSD drive.
2

Create Account

Once installed, launch the application and click on "Register New Pharmacy". You will need to provide the following information to verify your business:

  • Pharmacy Name and Address
  • Owner's National ID (NID)
  • Valid Drug License Number
  • Contact Phone Number

After submitting, you will receive an OTP (One Time Password) on your phone to verify your account. Once verified, you will be logged into the dashboard automatically.

3

Configure Settings

Before making your first sale, it's important to configure your store settings. Go to Settings > General to set up:

  • Currency & Tax: Set your default currency (BDT) and applicable VAT rates.
  • Receipt Header: Add your pharmacy logo and contact info for printed receipts.
  • User Management: Create accounts for your staff members and assign roles (Admin, Sales, Inventory).
  • Inventory Categories: Define categories like 'Antibiotics', 'Painkillers', etc., for better organization.
Pro Tip
Enable "Low Stock Alerts" in the Notification settings to get email or SMS warnings when medicine stock falls below your specified threshold.
