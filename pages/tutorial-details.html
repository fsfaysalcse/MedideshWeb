Complete Setup Guide: Getting Started with MediDesh

Beginner
12:45 minutes
8,234 views
Last updated: Oct 2025

Learn how to set up your pharmacy from scratch, configure essential settings, and process your first sale. This comprehensive guide will walk you through every step needed to get MediDesh running smoothly in your pharmacy.

Tutorial Steps

1

Download and Install MediDesh

Get started by downloading MediDesh for your preferred platform. We support Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, and iOS devices. Choose the version that matches your operating system and hardware requirements.

System Requirements
Minimum 4GB RAM, 2GB free disk space, and a stable internet connection. For optimal performance, we recommend 8GB RAM and an SSD drive.
  • Quick Installation
    Setup wizard guides you through the entire process in under 5 minutes
  • Automatic Updates
    Get the latest features and security patches automatically
  • Offline Mode
    Continue working even without internet connection
2

Create Your Account

Register your pharmacy by providing basic information including pharmacy name, license number, and contact details. This information will be used for invoices, reports, and regulatory compliance.

  • Pharmacy Information
    Name, address, license number, and tax registration details
  • Owner Details
    Name, email, phone number, and emergency contact
  • Security Settings
    Create a strong password and enable two-factor authentication
3

Configure System Settings

Set up essential system preferences including currency, tax rates, receipt formats, and business hours. These settings ensure MediDesh works perfectly for your specific needs.

  • Currency & Tax
    Set your local currency and configure VAT/GST rates
  • Receipt Customization
    Add your logo, customize layout, and set default messages
  • Business Hours
    Define opening hours and set up holiday schedules
Pro Tip
You can always change these settings later from the Settings menu. We recommend configuring everything during initial setup to avoid issues later.
4

Add Your First Products

Start building your inventory by adding medicines and products. You can add items individually or import them in bulk using our CSV template. Each product requires basic information like name, price, and stock quantity.

  • Product Information
    Name, generic name, manufacturer, and category
  • Pricing & Stock
    Purchase price, selling price, and current stock levels
  • Expiry Tracking
    Batch numbers and expiration dates for safety compliance
5

Connect Hardware Devices

Connect your barcode scanner, receipt printer, and cash drawer to streamline your workflow. MediDesh supports most standard USB and Bluetooth devices without requiring additional drivers.

  • Barcode Scanner
    USB or Bluetooth scanners for quick product lookup
  • Receipt Printer
    Thermal or inkjet printers for professional receipts
  • Cash Drawer
    Automatic opening with secure cash management
6

Process Your First Sale

You're ready to make your first sale! Use the POS interface to scan or search for products, adjust quantities, apply discounts, and process payments. The system will automatically update inventory and generate a receipt.

  • Add Products to Cart
    Scan barcodes or search by name to add items
  • Apply Discounts
    Percentage or fixed amount discounts per item or total
  • Accept Payment
    Cash, card, mobile payment, or split payment options
Congratulations!
You've completed the basic setup! Your pharmacy is now ready to handle sales. Explore advanced features like customer management, supplier tracking, and detailed reports from the main dashboard.
