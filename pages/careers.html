Our Hiring Process

What to expect when you apply

1

Application

Submit your application with your resume and cover letter. We review all applications within 5 business days.

2

Phone Screening

A 30-minute call with our HR team to discuss your background and interest in the role.

3

Technical Interview

For technical roles, a coding challenge or technical discussion with the engineering team.

4

Team Interview

Meet with team members and hiring managers to discuss the role and your fit with the team.

5

Offer

If successful, we'll extend an offer and welcome you to the Medidesh team!
