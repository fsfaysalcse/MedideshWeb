1. Introduction to GDPR Compliance

The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is a comprehensive data protection law that came into effect on May 25, 2018. MediDesh is committed to full compliance with GDPR principles and regulations.

This document explains how we comply with GDPR requirements and what rights you have regarding your personal data under GDPR.

GDPR Applies to You If

You are a resident of the European Union (EU) or European Economic Area (EEA), regardless of where you access our services.

1.1 GDPR Principles We Follow

Lawfulness

Data processed legally and transparently

Purpose Limitation

Collected for specific purposes only

Data Minimization

Only necessary data is collected

Accuracy

Data kept accurate and up-to-date

Storage Limitation

Retained only as long as necessary

Integrity & Confidentiality

Protected with appropriate security

3. Lawful Basis for Processing

We process your personal data under the following lawful bases as defined by GDPR Article 6:

3.1 Contract Performance

Processing is necessary to provide our pharmacy management services under our Terms of Service agreement with you.

  • Account creation and management
  • Service delivery and support
  • Payment processing
  • Communication regarding your account

3.2 Legitimate Interest

We have legitimate interests in:

  • Improving and developing our services
  • Detecting and preventing fraud
  • Ensuring network and information security
  • Internal administration and reporting

3.3 Legal Obligation

We process data to comply with legal requirements, including:

  • Tax and accounting obligations
  • Anti-money laundering regulations
  • Healthcare and pharmaceutical regulations
  • Court orders and law enforcement requests

3.4 Consent

For certain processing activities, we rely on your explicit consent:

  • Marketing communications
  • Non-essential cookies
  • Sharing data with third parties for non-essential purposes

4. Your GDPR Rights

Under GDPR, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

Right to Access

Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you, including information about how we use it.

Right to Rectification

Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data we hold about you.

Right to Erasure

Request deletion of your personal data (the "right to be forgotten") in certain circumstances.

Right to Restriction

Request that we restrict processing of your personal data in certain situations.

Right to Data Portability

Receive your personal data in a structured, machine-readable format and transfer it to another controller.

Right to Object

Object to processing of your personal data based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing.

Right to Withdraw Consent

Withdraw consent at any time where we rely on consent to process your data.

Rights Related to Automated Decision-Making

Not be subject to decisions based solely on automated processing that significantly affect you.

Exercising Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights, please contact our Data Protection Officer. We will respond within 30 days.

5. Data Processing Activities

We maintain a record of all processing activities as required by GDPR Article 30:

5.1 Categories of Personal Data

  • Identity Data: Name, username, title, date of birth
  • Contact Data: Email address, phone number, postal address
  • Financial Data: Payment card details, bank account information
  • Transaction Data: Purchase history, payment records
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, device information
  • Usage Data: How you use our services and website
  • Marketing Data: Communication preferences

5.2 Data Processors

We use the following categories of data processors:

  • Cloud hosting providers (AWS, Google Cloud)
  • Payment processors (Stripe)
  • Email service providers
  • Analytics providers (Google Analytics with anonymization)
  • Customer support platforms

All processors are bound by Data Processing Agreements (DPAs) that ensure GDPR compliance.

6. Data Protection Measures

We implement technical and organizational measures to ensure appropriate security:

Encryption in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256)
Role-based access controls and least privilege principle
Regular security audits and penetration testing
Data breach notification procedures within 72 hours
Staff training on data protection and GDPR compliance
Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIAs) for high-risk processing

7. International Data Transfers

We may transfer your personal data outside the EEA. When we do, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place:

7.1 Transfer Mechanisms

  • Adequacy Decisions: Transfers to countries with adequacy decisions from the EU Commission
  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs): EU-approved contract clauses with data processors
  • Binding Corporate Rules: Internal rules for multinational companies
  • Certification Mechanisms: EU-US Data Privacy Framework participation

7.2 Current Transfer Locations

We currently transfer data to the following regions with appropriate safeguards:

  • United States (AWS, Google Cloud) - Standard Contractual Clauses
  • Bangladesh (primary data storage) - Adequate data protection measures

8. Data Retention Periods

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes outlined:

8.1 Retention Schedule

  • Account Data: Duration of account + 30 days after closure
  • Transaction Records: 7 years (legal requirement)
  • Marketing Consent: Until consent is withdrawn + 30 days
  • Support Tickets: 3 years after resolution
  • Audit Logs: 1 year
  • Backup Data: 90 days

After the retention period, we securely delete or anonymize your personal data.

11. Submitting GDPR Requests

To exercise your GDPR rights or submit a request:

11.1 How to Submit a Request

  1. Email our DPO at dpo@medidesh.com
  2. Use the subject line "GDPR Request - [Type of Request]"
  3. Include your full name, email address, and account details
  4. Clearly state which right you wish to exercise
  5. Provide any additional information that may help us process your request

11.2 Request Processing

We will:

  • Acknowledge your request within 3 business days
  • Verify your identity before processing the request
  • Respond to your request within 30 days (may be extended by 60 days for complex requests)
  • Provide our response free of charge (unless requests are manifestly unfounded or excessive)

11.3 Verification Process

To protect your privacy, we may ask you to verify your identity by providing:

  • Government-issued ID
  • Proof of address
  • Account information only you would know
  • Two-factor authentication
