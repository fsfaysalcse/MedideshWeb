Our Story

Medidesh was born from a simple observation: while Bangladesh's pharmaceutical industry was thriving, most pharmacies were still managing their operations with outdated methods—paper records, manual inventory tracking, and basic cash registers.

Founded in July 2025 by a team of technology enthusiasts and healthcare professionals, we recognized the massive opportunity to digitize and modernize pharmacy operations across Bangladesh.

Starting with just three pharmacies in Dhaka, we're building solutions that are both powerful and accessible to pharmacies of all sizes across the country.

Our Mission

To democratize access to modern pharmacy management technology across Bangladesh, enabling every pharmacy—from small neighborhood stores to large chains—to operate efficiently, reduce waste, and provide better healthcare services to their communities.

Our Vision

To become the leading healthcare technology platform in Bangladesh, powering a connected ecosystem where pharmacies, healthcare providers, and patients benefit from seamless, intelligent, and accessible digital health solutions.

Our Values

Innovation, Integrity, and Impact guide everything we do. We believe in building technology that truly serves people, maintaining the highest standards of data security and privacy, and creating meaningful change in healthcare accessibility.

Our Journey

Key milestones in Medidesh's growth story

2021

Company Founded

Medidesh was established with a vision to digitize pharmacy operations across Bangladesh. Started with a small team of 4 people and our first prototype.

Early 2022

First Pilot Pharmacies

Launched our beta version with 3 pilot pharmacies in Dhaka. Gathered crucial feedback and refined our core POS and inventory features.

Mid 2022

Mobile App Launch

Released our first mobile applications for Android and iOS, making our platform accessible on smartphones and tablets.

Late 2022

Expansion to 50 Pharmacies

Achieved our first major milestone of serving 50+ pharmacies across 5 districts in Bangladesh, processing over 10,000 transactions monthly.

Early 2023

Advanced Analytics Launch

Introduced comprehensive reporting and analytics features, helping pharmacy owners make data-driven decisions about inventory and sales.

Mid 2023

Partnership with Major Suppliers

Formed strategic partnerships with leading pharmaceutical distributors in Bangladesh, enabling seamless integration and automated ordering.

Late 2023

Windows Desktop Application

Launched our Windows desktop application for comprehensive pharmacy management, featuring advanced reporting and multi-user capabilities.

2024

200+ Pharmacies Milestone

Crossed 200 active pharmacies and expanded to 12+ districts. Introduced batch tracking, expiry management, and intelligent alerts.

2025

Public Beta Launch

Preparing for our public beta launch in October 2025, with enhanced features, API access, and multi-location support for pharmacy chains.
